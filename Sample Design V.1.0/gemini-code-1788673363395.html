Location & Transit

Getting to Villa Cinnamoon Castle

Property Address & Coordinates

Villa Cinnamoon Castle
Arachchikanda, Hikkaduwa, Southern Province, Sri Lanka. Postal Code: 80240

GPS Coordinates: 6.1405° N, 80.1325° E

  • 1
    Via Southern Expressway (E01): Exit at Baddegama or Kurundugahahetekma interchange and head towards Hikkaduwa town. Take the Arachchikanda inland turnoff approximately 3.5 km before reaching the coastal strip.
  • 2
    Via Galle Road (A2 Coastal Highway): From Hikkaduwa main town / clock tower, head 3.5 km (approx. 5 minutes) inland toward the serene cinnamon grove landscape of Arachchikanda.
  • 3
    Arrival & Secure Gated Parking: Look for the Villa Cinnamoon Castle golden signpost. Enter through our private secure gated driveway with ample parking space for multiple cars, passenger vans, or tuk-tuks.
