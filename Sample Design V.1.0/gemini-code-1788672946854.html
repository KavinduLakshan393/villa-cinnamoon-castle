Location & Orientation

Arachchikanda, Hikkaduwa

Located 3.5 km inland from the lively Hikkaduwa coastal strip, surrounded by lush cinnamon groves, coconut palms, and tropical fruit trees. Offers complete peace and privacy away from crowds while remaining minutes from the ocean.

Easily accessible by car, van, or tuk-tuk with a secure gated driveway.
