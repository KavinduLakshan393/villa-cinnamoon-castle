Calendar Intelligence

Mixed Stay Split Pricing Formula

Stays spanning both Weekend nights (Fri, Sat, Sun) and Weekday nights (Mon–Thu) are computed transparently using our exact split formula with zero surprise surcharges.

How It Calculates

When your reservation includes nights from both classifications, the automated booking wizard divides the duration and multiplies each segment by its respective package rate:

Total Price = (N_weekend × Rate_weekend) + (N_weekday × Rate_weekday)

*Note: The maximum consecutive Weekday Stay is 4 nights (Mon–Thu). Any 5th consecutive night automatically crosses into Friday (Weekend).

Case Example 1: Friday to Tuesday (4 Nights)[cite: 4]
3 Weekend Nights @ Rs. 23,000 (A/C) = Rs. 69,000/=[cite: 4]
1 Weekday Night @ Rs. 19,900 (A/C) = Rs. 19,900/=[cite: 4]
Total Estimate = Rs. 88,900/=[cite: 4]
Case Example 2: Wednesday to Saturday (3 Nights)[cite: 4]

1 Weekend Night @ Rs. 21,000 (Non-A/C) = Rs. 21,000/=[cite: 4]
Total Estimate = Rs. 56,800/=[cite: 4]
