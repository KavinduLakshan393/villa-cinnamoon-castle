A private two-story sanctuary engineered for unhurried living.

Discover the intimate spaces of Villa Cinnamoon Castle. From antique-carved living halls to vaulted mezzanine breeze corridors, explore every sanctuary floor before you arrive.

Step 01 / Communal Spaces

Ground-Floor Living Hall

Step into an inviting reception space furnished with handcrafted Sri Lankan wooden armchairs and traditional caned seating. Equipped with a high-definition TV with satellite entertainment, this space provides a restful gathering spot after days on the beach.

Entertainment & Seating

Antique wooden suites • Satellite Television • Natural cross-ventilation

Upstairs Mezzanine Lounge

Ascend to the expansive mezzanine lounge characterized by high-vaulted exposed timber ceilings and open breeze corridors. Enjoy panoramic views of the surrounding palm canopy with comfortable sofa arrangements ideal for reading or group conversations.
