## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene, sun-dappled quietude of an inland Sri Lankan estate surrounded by cinnamon groves and architectural stone. Synthesizing the austere restraint of Aman Resorts with the polished discovery patterns of Airbnb Luxe, the interface balances editorial luxury and sensory transport. 

The aesthetic is grounded in organic minimalism and architectural tactility. Heavy spatial intervals, hairline frames, and museum-grade typography establish an unhurried, contemplative rhythm. The emotional response should be one of deep restorative calm, bespoke hospitality, and grounded warmth. Visual interactions rely on slow, confident choreographies: masked line reveals on scroll, whispered state transitions, and kinetic pill buttons that evoke hand-finished physical craft.

## Colors

The palette reflects the native earth, sun-dried spice bark, and architectural shade of the Arachchikanda landscape:

- **Primary (`#964B28` - Ceylon Bark):** Deep roasted cinnamon used for primary brand identifiers, editorial emphasis, signature CTAs, and active navigational moments.
- **Secondary (`#B85D36` - Sunlit Terracotta):** Warm fired clay for interactive hovers, supporting tags, accent lines, and highlight states.
- **Tertiary (`#D8A47F` - Raw Cinnamon Quill):** Soft, muted ochre undertone for subtle dividers, selection rings, and secondary chip outlines.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Coconut Milk / Warm Cream):** The luminous, low-strain backdrop that sets an unbleached organic tone. All light surfaces scale downward from this warm white.
- **Deep Architectural Night (`#0A0A0C`):** Dense charcoal-obsidian utilized for long-form reading, primary typography, high-contrast footer blocks, and full-bleed narrative overlays.
- **Surface Muted (`#F5EFE6`):** Tinted parchment for card backgrounds, secondary containers, and media plinths.
- **Border Hairline (`#E8DFD3`):** Delicate stone-chiseled borders providing definition without visual weight.

## Typography

The type system stages a dialogue between classical architectural gravitas and warm contemporary precision:

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Reserved for hero narrative titles, section anchors, quote callouts, and suite designations. It introduces high contrast, delicate serifs, and timeless resort grandeur. Key headings employ italicized accent words within lines to mimic high-end architectural monographs.
- **Humanist Geometric Grotesque (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for all transactional components, booking engines, metadata, body narratives, and interface controls. Its rounded forms bring soft modern clarity without clashing with the serif elegance.
- **Masked Line Reveals:** Display headings must be enclosed in overflow-hidden wrappers. When entering the viewport, lines translate vertically (`translateY(100%)` to `translateY(0%)`) with a bespoke cubic-bezier (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) over 900ms.
- **Labeling Standard:** All category headers, availability indicators, and location coordinates use `label-caps` set in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.14em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a rhythmic fixed-fluid grid with wide margins to create the breathable presence of a coffee table art book:

- **Desktop Structure:** 12-column grid anchored inside a maximum container width of `90rem` (`1440px`), with `3rem` (`48px`) outer margin padding and `2rem` (`32px`) gutters. Editorial reading panes are restricted to a centered `72rem` (`1152px`) grid.
- **Tablet / Reflow (768px - 1024px):** Transitions to an 8-column layout with `2rem` gutters and `2rem` outer padding. Two-column suite listings collapse into staggered full-width modules.
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Staggered imagery stacks sequentially; vertical line breaks transition from decorative side gutters to full-width horizontal separators.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Generous section spacing (`spacing-2xl` and `spacing-3xl`) separates experiential zones, enforcing contemplative reading without cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system replaces synthetic drop shadows with **tonal stratifications, warm ambient light diffusion, and hairline architectural boundaries**:

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is expressed primarily via surface warmth. Level 0 is the unbleached `#FDFBF7` canvas; Level 1 (cards, reservation pods) uses `#F5EFE6`; Level 2 (modals, luxury drawers) adopts pure `#FFFFFF` with a 1px boundary of `#E8DFD3`.
- **Sun-Filtered Ambient Shadows:** For floating elements (date-pickers, floating reservation bars), shadows utilize ultra-wide dispersion tinted with the primary cinnamon hue: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(150, 75, 40, 0.08), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.03)`.
- **Hairline Framing:** Architectural borders are strictly `1px solid #E8DFD3` or `rgba(150, 75, 40, 0.15)`. No heavy borders or stark black separators are permitted.
- **Curated Scrims:** Image modals and night-mode takeovers utilize deep architectural obsidian scrims at 85% opacity (`rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.85)`) with a subtle backdrop blur of `12px` to evoke dusk falling over the palm canopy.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines classical architectural rectangles with hyper-refined pill forms:

- **Structural Surfaces & Imagery:** Photographic assets, villa gallery frames, and ambient cards utilize architectural restraint: either razor-sharp (`0px`) or soft organic corners (`8px` max for cards). This preserves the dignity of architectural photography.
- **Interactive Affordances (Pills):** All interactive triggers—buttons, tags, date chips, and search bars—adhere to `roundedness: 3` (full pill geometry `rounded-full` / `9999px`). 
- **Kinetic Pill Interactions:** The contrast between architectural image boxes and curved kinetic pills creates a tactile, modern luxury juxtaposition.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Kinetic Pill Action (Primary):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), framed with a 1px border of `#964B28`, text in `#964B28` or `#0A0A0C` with `label-ui` sizing. Features a pseudo-element background fill in `#964B28` that scales/slides horizontally from left-to-right (`transform: scaleX(0)` to `scaleX(1)`, `transform-origin: left`) upon hover, transitioning text color to `#FDFBF7` seamlessly over 400ms.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Outlined pill using `#E8DFD3`, shifting to `#B85D36` on hover with a warm tinted background fill (`#F5EFE6`).
- **Text Link:** Serifs or uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with a trailing 1px cinnamon underline that expands from center-out on hover.

### 2. Cards (Suites & Experiences)
- Minimalist framing with `#F5EFE6` fill or transparent borderless layouts.
- Images maintain a 4:5 or 16:10 aspect ratio with an inner subtle zoom (`scale(1.03)`) on container hover over 700ms.
- Metadata (capacity, square footage, grove view) rendered using `label-caps` directly beneath the image, spaced with warm tertiary dots (`•`).

### 3. Chips & Filter Tags
- Pill-shaped (`padding: 8px 18px`), set in `label-caps`.
- Inactive state: `#FDFBF7` background, `1px solid #E8DFD3`, text `#0A0A0C`.
- Active state: `#0A0A0C` background, text `#FDFBF7`.

### 4. Form Fields & Date Range Selectors
- Minimalist underline or boxed hairline style (`1px solid #E8DFD3`).
- Backgrounds remain `#FDFBF7` or translucent.
- On focus: bottom or outer border transitions smoothly to `#964B28` with zero outer neon glow; replaced instead by a slight label shift upwards in `label-caps` styling.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom circular radios and delicate square checkboxes with 2px corner radius.
- Unchecked: `1px solid #D8A47F`.
- Checked: `#964B28` solid fill with an unbleached `#FDFBF7` check icon or inner dot.

### 6. Signature Domain Components
- **Masked Editorial Scroller:** Section titles that trigger individual line reveals on intersection observation.
- **Bespoke Concierge Bar:** Floating bottom pill housing dates, guest counter, and booking trigger, fixed dynamically with diffused cinnamon drop shadow and translucent cream glass (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.92)` backdrop blur).
- **Botanical Story Carousel:** Full-bleed image transition component with vertical counter (`01 / 06`) in `Playfair Display` italics.
