## 2. Editorial direction

The website should not behave like a property-listing gallery. Media should be selected to tell one clear story:

1. A private villa surrounded by tropical greenery
2. Space for families and groups to stay together
3. Comfortable sleeping arrangements presented as one unified experience
4. Shared living and dining
5. Real guest warmth, only where publication consent is confirmed
6. A small, carefully selected view of nearby Hikkaduwa experiences

The five bedrooms must never be presented as separate website categories such as “Bedroom 1”, “Bedroom 2”, etc. Selected bedroom images should appear together in one editorial composition without room numbering.

## 4. Current media limitations

- Most property images are portrait-oriented and between roughly 720–1200 pixels wide.
- There is no strong, professionally composed horizontal exterior photograph suitable for a conventional full-screen desktop hero.
- No pool photography is present; the website must not imply that the villa has a pool.
- Several interiors show exposed wiring, temporary equipment, packaging or incomplete styling.
- Some bedroom images include a phone-camera watermark and should not be used.
- Many guest images are casual selfies. They are valuable as authentic social proof but should not dominate the core property presentation.

## 5. Video decision

The current 28 videos are primarily portrait handheld walkthroughs at approximately 480 pixels wide. They are not suitable for a cinematic desktop hero or large background video.

Phase 1 recommendation: **do not use the current videos as hero or full-width background media**. A still-image editorial composition will look more deliberate and professional.

If a small mobile-only story clip is later required, `VID-20260209-WA0119.mp4` is the most promising greenery/balcony source, but it still requires stabilisation, colour correction, careful trimming and silent autoplay treatment.

## 6. Recommended future photo/video capture

Before the final visual build, a short horizontal reshoot would materially improve the website:

- Wide exterior arrival shot in soft morning or late-afternoon light
- Wide interior shot showing the connected shared spaces
- One composed sleeping-experience image with finished styling and no exposed wiring
- Natural group dining moment without visible product branding
- 8–12 second stabilised landscape video moving from greenery toward the villa

This is an enhancement, not a blocker. The Phase 1 design can proceed with the shortlisted still images using an asymmetrical editorial layout instead of a conventional full-width hero photograph.
