Location & Atmosphere

Nestled just minutes from Hikkaduwa’s golden coastline, our spacious two-story haven offers complete privacy surrounded by whispering cinnamon groves and lush palms.

The Story Behind Our Stillness

Villa Cinnamoon Castle was born from a simple desire: to create a generous, unhurried space for families and groups seeking tranquility away from crowded beachfront strips.

Featuring five expansive bedrooms with handcrafted wooden beds, hot water amenities, and a fully equipped kitchen, guests enjoy exclusive, 100% private use of the entire estate.

Step outside into a private gravel courtyard enclosed by rustic timber fencing—ideal for leisurely afternoon teas, evening BBQ dinners, and shared moments under the stars.

The Living Experience

We believe in holiday spaces that speak softly. Where uncrowded nature, warm hospitality, and timeless architecture create an effortless sense of home.
